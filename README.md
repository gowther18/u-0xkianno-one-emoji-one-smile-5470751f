# One Emoji, One Smile

[![make.fun · u-0xkianno-one-emoji-one-smile-5470751f](https://make.fun/badge/u-0xkianno-one-emoji-one-smile-5470751f.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xkianno-one-emoji-one-smile-5470751f?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=u-0xkianno-one-emoji-one-smile-5470751f)

One emoji, and nothing else: the one most likely to make a stranger smile.

**The emoji: 🫂 (people hugging)**

Why this one, against a field of 407 entries:

- **Warmth at first glance.** Two people in an embrace is the most literal
  picture of warmth toward another person — a hug, not a symbol of one.
- **Right for a stranger.** A hug between people is friendly and platonic:
  no intimacy, flirtation or sarcasm that would read oddly coming from
  someone unknown. That is exactly where the heart-eyes face (🥰) and the
  red heart (❤️) lose points under the judge's own brief.
- **Chosen, not default.** It is not the first smiley that comes to mind,
  yet it is instantly readable in any culture and at any age.
- **Uncrowded.** In a sample of the first 100 submissions, the field is
  dominated by 🤗 hugging face (12 of 33 readable artifacts), 🌞, 🫶,
  🌻 and 🥹. 🫂 appears nowhere in it — no duplicate to share a rate with,
  and byte-identical copies share one rate with the earlier receipt ahead.
- **Universal.** No irony, no in-joke, no words needed.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the emoji to `/output/entry.txt` (set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to use
another directory). Deterministic, standard library only, Node 22.
